# Adventure Awaits Game Manual

---

## Startup 🧐

---
Start the game via an IDE, there is not an included JAR file for this project yet. When the game starts
you will be asked to create a weapon and a player. Type in the desired names for each and the game vill begin.

## Menu

---

## "[1] - 🗺️ Go on adventure"

---

This will start the actual GamePlay loop. Here you get to pick a location and either a treasure or a monster will 
present itself.

## "[2] - 🧝🏻 Show info about player"

---

Simply displays statistics about your character.

## "[3] - 🛖 Go to the Shop"

---

A place where you can use your gold to recover your HP or buy valuable upgrades that 
makes you significantly stronger.

## "[4] - 🎮 Change difficulty"

---

You can change the Difficulty in this menu. There are three Difficulties to choose from.

* 😈 Easy - sets monster power to 50% and Escape chance to 75%
* 👿 Medium - sets monster power to 100% and Escape chance to 50%
* 💀 Hard - sets monster power to 150% and Escape chance to 25%

## Battle/BattleHUD "[1] 🗡️ [2] 🧪 [0] 🥾"

---

There are three Different Actions you can use in Battle.

1. 🗡️ Choose one to attack the enemy with your weapon.
2. 🧪 Choose two to use a potion to recover all your HP. These are limited but can be cheaply acquired in the Shop.
3. 🥾 Choosing zero means you try to escape the battle, the chance of success depends on the set difficulty. 

## Milestones

---

There are three separate Milestones that can be attained in the game. I don't want to spoil them here. 
Play the game, they will Log on the screen when you reach them. 

## PowerUp

---

There are three ways to get more powerful. 

* Level Up. When you get enough experience from fighting monsters you level up and your Weapons damage increase with 1 and your HP with 10.
* Weapon Upgrade. Can be purchased in the Shop repeatably and increase your weapons damage with 1. 
* Enchantments. Powerful enchantments that are expensive and can only be purchased once. They are very powerful.

## Objective

---

Your objective is to survive until you reach level 10. You will lose the progress and have to start over if you are killed
in battle. Keep potions on you at all times.
